# 🌱 ECO-PULSE — Personal Carbon Intelligence

> **Measure your footprint. Understand your impact. Make better choices.**

ECO-PULSE is a personal carbon intelligence platform designed to help individuals understand the environmental impact of their everyday activities. It transforms daily lifestyle choices into meaningful carbon insights, helping users track emissions, explore sustainable alternatives, and build greener habits.

Instead of simply showing a carbon footprint, ECO-PULSE helps users understand **where their emissions come from and what they can do to reduce them.**

---

## 🌍 Overview

Our everyday decisions — how we travel, what we eat, how much electricity we consume, what we purchase, and how we manage waste — contribute to our carbon footprint.

ECO-PULSE brings these activities together into a unified platform that enables users to:

- Track estimated daily carbon emissions.
- Identify their major emission sources.
- Understand trends in their environmental impact.
- Simulate alternative lifestyle choices.
- Build sustainable habits through interactive missions.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Carbon Dashboard
Visualize daily emissions, historical trends, category breakdowns, and progress over time through an interactive dashboard.

### 📝 Daily Check-In
Record everyday activities across five major emission categories:

- 🚗 Transport
- 🍽️ Food
- ⚡ Electricity
- 🛍️ Purchases
- ♻️ Waste

### 🎙️ ORA — Multilingual Voice Assistant
An interactive conversational assistant that guides users through their daily carbon check-in using voice or text, with multilingual interaction designed for English, Kannada, and Hindi.

### 🔬 What-If Carbon Simulator
Experiment with alternative daily choices and compare estimated emissions to understand the potential environmental savings before making a change.

### 🧠 Eco Insights
Understand personal emission patterns, identify high-impact categories, and receive practical, personalized sustainability suggestions.

### 🌳 Impact Tree & Missions
Stay motivated through sustainability missions, streaks, XP, progress tracking, and virtual environmental growth.

### 🏆 Eco League
An optional community experience where users can compare sustainability progress while keeping personal activity records private.

### 🔐 Privacy & User Control
Designed around personal data ownership, secure authentication, and user control over their information.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Record** — Enter daily lifestyle activities manually or through ORA voice interaction.
2. **Calculate** — Estimate emissions using activity-based carbon calculation logic.
3. **Visualize** — Explore your carbon footprint through dashboards and category breakdowns.
4. **Analyze** — Identify your largest emission contributors and trends.
5. **Simulate** — Compare alternative habits and evaluate potential emission reductions.
6. **Improve** — Follow missions and make sustainable changes over time.

---

## 💡 Example

Instead of simply recording:

> "I travelled 15 km by motorcycle today."

ECO-PULSE helps you explore:

> "What if I used public transport for the same journey?"

The simulator estimates the difference between the two scenarios and helps you understand the potential reduction in emissions.
